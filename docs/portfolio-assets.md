### The 2-Minute Pitch (The "Interview Answer")
"For my capstone project, I wanted to tackle a real-world problem in EdTech. Traditional platforms like Canvas or Blackboard are very static. I built StudyOS from scratch using Python, Django, and PostgreSQL to make learning dynamic.

I hooked up the Google Gemini API to automate the heavy lifting. Teachers can upload a syllabus PDF, and the system automatically parses the text and generates a structured Multiple Choice Quiz. 

For students, I built a Career Assistant module. They upload their resume, the AI gives them an ATS score, and then generates a personalized roadmap to their dream job. The entire system is deployed on Render with secure authentication and environment variables."
